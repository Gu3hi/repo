01FEATURES · 功能
01

连接与电源Connectivity and power

在连接模块内右滑切换到电源控制；长按支持的模块打开二级页面。可选用个人热点替换隔空投送按钮。

Swipe right inside Connectivity to reveal power controls. Hold supported modules to open their detailed controls. Personal Hotspot can optionally replace the AirDrop button.

02

亮度、音量与音乐Brightness, volume and music

调整亮度与音量，查看音乐信息与控制播放；可使用当前封面作为音乐模块背景。

Adjust brightness and volume, view music information and control playback. Optionally use current artwork as the music module background.

03

电池、日历、提醒事项与天气Battery, calendar, reminders and weather

四个信息卡片显示设备电量、近期日程、待办提醒和当前天气，轻点打开详情浮窗。日历与提醒事项只读取已授权的数据，锁屏时隐藏内容。

Four info cards show device battery levels, upcoming events, reminders and current weather. Tap one for a detail popover. Calendar and Reminders read only data you have authorized and hide their contents on the Lock Screen.

04

快捷指令第二页Shortcuts on a second page

在面板空白或边缘横滑切换页面。从手机已有快捷指令中选择，系统负责执行；快捷页两列最多显示四行，更多内容上下滚动。

Swipe across blank panel space or its edge to switch pages. Choose existing shortcuts on your iPhone and let the system run them. The quick page shows two columns and up to four visible rows, with vertical scrolling for overflow.

05

2×1 快捷按钮（测试中）2×1 quick buttons (Beta)

宽按钮只显示图标，可独立启用与排序；与快捷指令共用第二页，支持范围以设置中的可选项目为准。

Icon-only wide buttons can be enabled and reordered on the second page alongside shortcuts. Available actions are those offered in Settings.

06

配色、主题与玻璃Colors, themes and glass

配色预设、明暗外观、自定义背景、系统图标与静态／动态图标主题均可配置。面板、模块、信息卡片、滑块与封面圆角可分别调节，默认使用连续圆角，内层圆角随外框同心计算。玻璃效果默认关闭，可独立启用；这些功能无需激活码。

Configure color presets, light and dark appearance, custom backgrounds, system icons and static or animated icon themes. Panel, module, info card, slider and artwork corner radii are adjustable separately, with continuous corners by default and inner corners kept concentric with their container. Glass is optional and disabled by default. These features do not require an activation code.

07

首次展开与功能导览Initial expansion and feature tour

默认从左右两侧底部上滑呼出，中间保留 Home 手势。可选择首次显示 1、2、3 个或全部控制区块，二次上拉展开；首次空配置提供可跳过、可重放的三步手势引导。

Open from either bottom corner by default, leaving the middle for the Home gesture. Show one, two, three or all control sections initially, then pull up again to expand. Fresh configurations receive a skippable three-gesture tour that can be replayed in Settings.

功能范围 Scope

这是浮动控制中心插件，不替代锁屏通知播放器，也不提供跨 App 全局玻璃。快捷指令执行能力取决于 iOS 和指令自身；需要交互的指令可能显示系统提示或打开快捷指令 App。Vela Studio 是独立配套 App，不包含在本插件安装包中。

This is a floating Control Center tweak. It does not replace the Lock Screen notification player or provide glass throughout other apps. Shortcut execution depends on iOS and each shortcut; interactive shortcuts may show system prompts or open the Shortcuts app. Vela Studio is a separate companion app and is not included in these packages.

安装与验证状态 Installation and testing

最低部署版本为 iOS 16。按实际越狱环境选择一个包：RootHide 为 iphoneos-arm64e，Rootless 为 iphoneos-arm64，不可混装。安装后前往「设置 → Vela」配置；已有用户保存的呼出设置继续生效。

The minimum deployment target is iOS 16. Install one package matching your jailbreak: iphoneos-arm64e for RootHide, or iphoneos-arm64 for Rootless. Configure Settings → Vela after installation. Existing saved activation gestures are preserved.

RootHide 与 Rootless 双包均包含 arm64 和 arm64e 切片，并分别构建验包。RootHide 包已在 iOS 17 真机上查看圆角效果；Rootless 与 iOS 16 尚未真机测试。编译和验包不能证明所有 iOS 版本的兼容性。

The RootHide and Rootless packages each contain arm64 and arm64e slices and are built and inspected separately. The RootHide package has been checked on an iOS 17 device for the corner changes; Rootless and iOS 16 have not been tested on device. Build and package checks do not establish compatibility on every iOS version.

02RELEASE · 本次更新

0.4.32 · 2026-10-31

0.4.32 汇总 0.4.21 以来的更新：新增电池、日历、提醒事项与天气信息卡片及详情浮窗，天气在系统数据过期时直连 Open-Meteo 获取；连接模块可用个人热点替换隔空投送；圆角统一为连续圆角并按内外同心计算，「模块圆角」同时控制卡片、按钮及其上色层、滑块与音乐进度，信息卡片圆角单独设置；修正设置页分组圆角与分隔线。

Version 0.4.32 collects the changes since 0.4.21: battery, calendar, reminders and weather info cards with detail popovers, with weather fetched from Open-Meteo when system data is stale; Personal Hotspot can replace AirDrop in Connectivity; corners are continuous and concentric, Module Corner Radius now drives cards, buttons and their tint layers, sliders and music progress, and info cards have their own radius setting; Settings group corners and separators are fixed.
