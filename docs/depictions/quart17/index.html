LOCK SCREEN, REIMAGINED

Quart17

2.7.112Put_StoryiOS 17

Quart17 在 iOS 17 上实现 iOS 27 的小时间样式，并为越狱设备提供可定制的锁屏音乐播放器、专辑封面、通知排版与缩放，以及下拉清理手势。

Quart17 brings the iOS 27-style compact Lock Screen clock to iOS 17, alongside a customizable music player, album artwork, notification layouts and scaling, and pull-to-clear gestures for jailbroken devices.

2.7.1122026-10-26＋

紧凑播放器迁移到 MediaRemoteUI 通知列表场景，修复媒体宿主识别、原生进度拖动、三种进度样式及播放器开关与圆角的跨进程同步；关闭通知样式后恢复原生通知大小并隐藏计数。RootHide 经用户真机测试确认；rootless 已独立构建验包，尚未真机测试。本版最低系统为 iOS 17。

Move the compact player into MediaRemoteUI's notification list scene; fix media-host identification, native seeking, three progress styles, and cross-process player switches and corners. Disabling notification styling restores native sizes and hides counts. RootHide testing was confirmed by the user; rootless is independently built and inspected, with device testing pending. This release requires iOS 17.
